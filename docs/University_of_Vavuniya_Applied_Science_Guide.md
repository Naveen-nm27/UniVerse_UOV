# University of Vavuniya — Faculty of Applied Science

## Degree programmes, course modules and Honours entry

**Prepared:** 1 October 2026  
**Scope:** The Faculty of Applied Science programmes discussed in our conversation; this is not a catalogue of every faculty at the university.

This guide consolidates the earlier information and links to official university sources. Module lists for IT and AMC/Computer Science are organised by level and semester. The Environmental Science section includes the subject areas and module examples previously discussed, with a link to the full official syllabus.

## 1. Departments and degrees

The Faculty of Applied Science has two departments: Physical Science and Bio-Science. Its five undergraduate degree programmes are:

| Department | Degree programme | Normal duration |
|---|---|---|
| Physical Science | BSc in Applied Mathematics and Computing | 3 years |
| Physical Science | BSc Honours in Computer Science | 4 years |
| Physical Science | BSc in Information Technology | 3 years |
| Physical Science | BSc Honours in Information Technology | 4 years |
| Bio-Science | BSc Honours in Environmental Science | 4 years |

Source: [Faculty academic programmes](https://fas.vau.ac.lk/academic-programme/).

### Main subject areas

| Area | Main focus |
|---|---|
| Information Technology | Programming, web technologies, databases, networking, security, software engineering and applied computing |
| Computer Science | Algorithms, programming, computing theory, operating systems, artificial intelligence, machine learning and cryptography |
| Applied Mathematics and Computing | Applied mathematics, statistics and computer science |
| Environmental Science | Biology, chemistry, climate, natural resources and environmental management |

**Terminology:** A level normally represents one academic year. Each level has two semesters. A degree programme is the qualification pathway; a module or course unit is an individual subject within it. Auxiliary courses include English and other supporting skills courses.

## 5. How Honours entry works

Honours entry happens at different stages for these programmes. It should not be understood as a universal process of graduating with a general degree and then returning for a separate Honours qualification.

Source: [Official Honours selection requirements](https://fas.vau.ac.lk/dps/honours-degree-selection/).

### 5.1 Applied Mathematics and Computing → Computer Science Honours

Students apply **after completing Level 2**, before completing the full three-year general route. Selected students begin the Computer Science Honours curriculum in **Level 3 Semester 1**.

Eligibility requirements:

- At least **C- in every core course** in Levels 1 and 2, covering **60 core credits**.
- At least **C in English Language I and English Language II**.
- At least **D+ in the other auxiliary courses** in Levels 1 and 2.
- **Overall Grade Point Average (OGPA) of at least 3.00** for Levels 1 and 2.

**Pathway:** Level 1 → Level 2 → application and selection → Level 3 Computer Science Honours → Level 4 → BSc Honours in Computer Science.

Students who do not enter the Honours pathway normally continue to Level 3 of BSc Applied Mathematics and Computing, subject to the programme's progression and award requirements.

### 5.2 Information Technology → IT Honours

Students apply **at the end of Level 3**. Selection considers academic performance across **Levels 1, 2 and 3**. Selected students continue to Level 4.

Eligibility requirements:

- At least **C- in every core course** across Levels 1–3.
- At least **C in English Language I and English Language II**.
- At least **D+ in the other auxiliary courses** across Levels 1–3.
- **OGPA of at least 3.00** for Levels 1–3.

**Pathway:** Level 1 → Level 2 → Level 3 → application and selection → Level 4 → BSc Honours in Information Technology.

Students who do not continue to Honours can receive the three-year BSc IT if they satisfy its award requirements. Completing Level 3 does not by itself mean the degree has already been formally awarded before Honours continuation.

**Meeting the 3.00 threshold does not guarantee selection.** The university states that a selected number of IT students continue based on academic performance. The cited page does not give a fixed number of places or a higher competitive cutoff.

### 5.3 Environmental Science

Students entering the Department of Bio-Science follow the **four-year Honours programme from admission**. The cited degree page does not describe a separate competitive Honours application after Level 2 or Level 3 comparable to IT or Computer Science.

**Pathway:** Admission → Level 1 → Level 2 → Level 3 → Level 4 → BSc Honours in Environmental Science.

The programme page specifies at least 120 credits and auxiliary course passes. It gives **C or above for English I and II** and **D or above for other auxiliary courses** as completion prerequisites. These Environmental Science conditions differ from the **D+** requirement stated for IT/CS Honours selection.

### 5.4 Comparison

| Starting programme | Honours application stage | Minimum selection OGPA | Continuation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Applied Mathematics and Computing | After Level 2 | 3.00 for Levels 1–2 | Computer Science Honours in Levels 3–4 |
| Information Technology | At the end of Level 3 | 3.00 for Levels 1–3 | IT Honours in Level 4 |
| Environmental Science | Direct four-year Honours route | No separate selection threshold stated on the cited programme page | Levels 1–4 of Environmental Science Honours |

For IT and Computer Science, the university describes 120 core credits over the four years, including a 6-credit research project, with 6-credit industrial training and 16 credits of auxiliary courses also offered.

### 5.5 Practical application process

The published rules identify when students apply and the academic requirements. Students should follow their department's application notice and form, submit within the announced deadline, and await the selection decision. The sources cited here do not establish a current deadline, seat count, interview requirement or appeal procedure.

## 6. Opting for a three-year degree during Honours

For Physical Science programmes, section 3.1.7 of the Faculty Handbook 2023/2024 permits a fourth-year Honours student to request the three-year bachelor's degree **before submitting the research thesis**, with a valid reason. Acceptance requires Faculty Board recommendation and Senate approval.

The Environmental Science programme page also describes a fourth-year option to request a general degree before thesis submission. That webpage uses older institutional wording, including “Campus Board”. Students should use the applicable Faculty handbook and obtain confirmation of the current approval process.

Source: [Faculty Handbook 2023/2024](https://fas.vau.ac.lk/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/FAS-Handbook-2023-2024.pdf).
